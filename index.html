Know Your Rights

Fundamental constitutional rights and public service guarantees for Karnataka citizens.

gavel

Anti-Corruption & Transparency

Citizens should not be required to make unlawful payments to access public services. Report bribery or official extortion safely with Privacy Shield protection.

Report Issue arrow_forward
description

Right to Information (RTI)

Every citizen has the legal right to request government records, municipal expenditure logs, and infrastructure project allocation data under RTI 2005.

Explore Data arrow_forward
verified

Public Services Delivery

Under Sakala (Karnataka Guarantee of Services to Citizens Act), civic services such as clean water, road maintenance, and waste clearance must meet time-bound SLA timelines.

Right to Clean Environment

Article 21 guarantees the right to a clean, unpolluted environment including solid waste clearance, safe drainage, and unblocked footpaths.
